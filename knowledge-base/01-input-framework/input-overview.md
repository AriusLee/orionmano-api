# Input Framework Overview

The Orionmano AI advisory platform collects customer data progressively across 3 engagement stages. Each stage unlocks specific deliverables. Data is collected via structured forms, document uploads (PDF parsing), and management interviews.

## Progressive Data Collection

| Stage | Duration | Data Collected | Deliverables Unlocked |
|-------|----------|---------------|----------------------|
| 1 - Onboarding | 30-60 min | Company profile, corporate structure, leadership, products/services, 2-year audited financials, management accounts | **Gap Analysis**, Sales Deck, Kick-off Deck |
| 2 - Financial Deep Dive | 2-4 hours | Tax filings, contracts, legal docs, interview materials | Industry Report, DD Report |
| 3 - Market Ready | 4-8 hours | Projections, comparable data, investor materials, governance docs | Valuation Report, Teaser, Company Deck |

> **Gap Analysis** is the first deliverable produced — typically between the first and second client meetings. It requires only the 4 key documents: (1) corporate proposal/profile, (2) 2-year audited financial statements, (3) current management reports, and (4) shareholder structure & cap table. This is the most frequently used module and determines whether to proceed with a full engagement.

## Input Sources

### Documents (uploaded & AI-parsed)
- Prospectus / Draft registration statement
- Audited financial statements (2-3 years)
- Management accounts / interim financials
- Tax returns and correspondence with authorities
- Material contracts (customer, supplier, bank facilities)
- Legal opinions and litigation documents
- Corporate structure charts
- Interview transcripts / management representations
- Board resolutions and shareholder agreements
- IP registrations and licenses

### Structured Data (form input)
- Company profile fields (name, registration, incorporation date, jurisdiction)
- Shareholder register and equity structure
- Revenue breakdown by segment/customer/geography
- Cost structure breakdown
- Key personnel profiles
- Management projections and budgets

### External Research (AI-gathered)
- Industry market data (market size, growth rates, key players)
- Comparable company financials and multiples
- Regulatory environment and recent changes
- Macroeconomic indicators relevant to the industry

## Data Quality Requirements

- **Audited financials** must be from a recognized audit firm with clean or qualified opinion noted
- **Management accounts** should be clearly labeled as unaudited
- **Projections** must include stated assumptions
- **All data** should specify the reporting currency and period
- **Square brackets [ ]** are used in draft reports for items requiring client clarification or confirmation
