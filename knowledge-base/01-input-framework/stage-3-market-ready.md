# Stage 3: Market Ready — Valuation & Investor Materials

**Duration:** 4-8 hours
**Deliverables Unlocked:** Valuation Report, Company Teaser, Company Deck

## Required Information

### 1. Financial Projections
- 3-5 year financial projections (P&L, BS, CF)
- Key assumptions document:
  - Revenue growth drivers and assumptions by segment
  - Margin assumptions and basis
  - Capital expenditure plans
  - Working capital assumptions
  - Funding requirements and sources
- Sensitivity analysis on key variables
- Management's base, upside, and downside scenarios

### 2. Comparable Company Data
- List of peer/comparable companies identified by management
- Industry classification and SIC/NAICS codes
- Publicly available comparable company financials (AI will also research)
- Recent M&A transactions in the sector (if known)

### 3. Market & Industry Data
- Industry reports or market research (if available from management)
- Total addressable market (TAM) estimates
- Market growth projections
- Competitive landscape assessment
- Regulatory outlook

### 4. Valuation-Specific Information
- Any prior valuations performed (internal or external)
- Recent funding rounds and implied valuations
- Any indicative offers or expressions of interest received
- Target valuation range (if management has a view)
- Discount rate or WACC inputs:
  - Risk-free rate reference
  - Beta and comparable company beta data
  - Country risk premium
  - Size premium
  - Company-specific risk factors

### 5. IPO / Capital Market Readiness
- Target exchange and market tier
- Proposed offering structure (primary, secondary, size)
- Use of proceeds plan
- Lock-up arrangements
- Professional team:
  - Lead underwriter / sponsor
  - Legal counsel (domestic and international)
  - Auditor
  - Company secretary
  - Valuer (if independent valuation required)
- Timeline and key milestones

### 6. Governance & Compliance
- Board composition and independence
- Board committees (audit, nomination, remuneration)
- Corporate governance policies
- Internal control framework documentation
- Code of conduct and whistleblower policy
- ESG/sustainability report or initiatives
- SOX readiness assessment (for US listings)
- Sarbanes-Oxley 302/404 compliance status

### 7. Investor Materials (existing, if any)
- Existing investor presentations or pitch decks
- Information memorandum (IM) or business plan
- Non-disclosure agreement (NDA) template
- Term sheets from prior negotiations
- Investor FAQ or Q&A preparation documents
