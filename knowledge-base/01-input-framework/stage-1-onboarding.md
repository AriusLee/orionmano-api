# Stage 1: Onboarding — Basic Company Profile

**Duration:** 30-60 minutes
**Deliverables Unlocked:** **Gap Analysis**, Sales Deck, Kick-off Meeting Deck

## Required Information

### 1. Company Profile
- Legal name (and trading/brand name if different)
- Company registration number
- Date and jurisdiction of incorporation
- Company type (private limited, public, LLP, etc.)
- Registered address and principal place of business
- Website URL
- Brief description of business (1-2 paragraphs)

### 2. Corporate Structure
- Group structure diagram (parent, subsidiaries, associates)
- Ownership percentages for each entity
- Inter-company relationships and transaction flows
- Jurisdictions of each entity in the group
- Any offshore holding entities (Cayman, BVI, etc.)

### 3. Shareholding & Equity
- Current shareholder register (name, shares, percentage, class)
- Share classes and voting rights (ordinary, preference, etc.)
- Any convertible instruments (RCPS, convertible loans)
- Share price history and recent transactions
- Acting-in-concert agreements or shareholder agreements

### 4. Key Personnel
- Founder(s) background and role
- C-suite team (CEO, CFO, COO, CTO) with brief bios
- Board of directors composition
- Key management personnel
- Any recent senior hires or departures

### 5. Business Overview
- Principal business activities (brief)
- Products and services offered
- Key customer segments
- Geographic markets served
- Competitive positioning (self-described)

### 6. Engagement Context
- Purpose of engagement (IPO preparation, M&A, fundraising, compliance)
- Target exchange or market (if IPO)
- Target timeline
- Underwriter / sponsor / advisor (if already appointed)
- Specific areas of concern flagged by management

## Documents to Upload

### Required for Gap Analysis (minimum 4 documents)
These are the minimum documents needed to produce the Gap Analysis report:
1. **Corporate proposal / company profile** — business overview, history, key personnel, org structure
2. **2 years audited financial statements** — primary source for all financial metrics
3. **Current management reports** — latest unaudited balance sheet and P&L
4. **Shareholder structure & cap table** — equity ownership, voting rights, related parties

### Additional Documents (for Sales Deck, Kick-off Deck)
- Corporate structure chart (if available)
- Certificate of incorporation
- Memorandum & Articles of Association (M&A)
- Recent board resolutions (if relevant)
