# Stage 2: Financial Deep Dive

**Duration:** 2-4 hours
**Deliverables Unlocked:** Industry Expert Report, Due Diligence Report

## Required Information

### 1. Audited Financial Statements (2-3 years)
- Complete audited financial statements (P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Notes)
- Auditor's report and opinion type (unqualified, qualified, adverse, disclaimer)
- Management letter / auditor's management letter (if available)
- Trial balance (for reconciliation)

### 2. Income Statement Detail
- Revenue breakdown by:
  - Product/service line
  - Customer (top 5-10 by value)
  - Geographic segment
  - Revenue type (recurring/subscription vs. one-off)
- Cost of revenue / COGS breakdown by major category
- Operating expense breakdown (salaries, rent, marketing, depreciation, etc.)
- Other income and losses detail
- Finance costs breakdown (interest by facility, lease costs)
- Tax computation and reconciliation

### 3. Balance Sheet Detail
- Accounts receivable aging schedule
- Inventory breakdown and aging (if applicable)
- Property, plant & equipment schedule (additions, disposals, depreciation)
- Intangible assets detail and amortization
- Right-of-use assets and lease liability schedules
- Bank borrowings schedule (facility, amount, rate, maturity, covenants)
- Convertible instrument terms and conditions
- Related party balances and transactions
- Equity movement schedule

### 4. Cash Flow Detail
- Capital expenditure detail
- Working capital movement analysis
- Debt drawdown and repayment schedule
- Dividend history

### 5. Management Accounts
- Latest available management accounts (unaudited)
- Monthly/quarterly P&L for current period
- Budget vs. actual variance analysis

### 6. Tax Information
- Corporate income tax returns (2-3 years)
- Tax assessment notices and correspondence with authorities
- Transfer pricing documentation (if inter-company transactions exist)
- Tax loss carryforward schedule
- Withholding tax obligations
- GST/VAT/SST registration and compliance status

### 7. Key Contracts
- Top 5 customer contracts (terms, duration, value)
- Top 5 supplier contracts
- Bank facility agreements and covenants
- Lease agreements (property, equipment)
- License/franchise agreements
- Insurance policies

### 8. Legal
- Legal proceedings (pending, threatened, or concluded in last 3 years)
- Regulatory compliance status
- Intellectual property registrations
- Material litigation risk assessment from legal counsel

### 9. Interview Materials
- Management interview transcripts or notes covering:
  - Strategic vision and business development
  - Operational workflow and processes
  - Customer acquisition and retention strategy
  - Technology roadmap
  - Governance and internal controls
  - ESG initiatives
- Customer and supplier interview summaries (top 5-10 representing 60%+ of revenue/purchases)

## Key Focus Areas for Analysis
- Accounts receivable adequacy and ECL provisioning
- PPE and equipment valuation and impairment
- Revenue recognition policies and sustainability
- Lease accounting treatment (MFRS 16 / IFRS 16 / ASC 842)
- Related party transaction pricing and disclosure
- Debt classification and covenant compliance
- Working capital trends and liquidity assessment
