# Market Analysis Framework

This framework provides detailed guidance on how to conduct market sizing, competitive analysis, and trend identification for the Industry Expert Report.

## Market Sizing Methodology

### Top-Down Approach
1. Start with global market size from reputable research firms
2. Narrow to regional market using geographic share data
3. Further narrow to specific segment/niche relevant to the company
4. Calculate company's addressable market and implied market share

### Bottom-Up Validation
1. Estimate total number of potential customers in target market
2. Multiply by average spend per customer
3. Apply adoption/penetration rate
4. Cross-reference with top-down estimate

### Data Presentation
- Always present: **Current Value** (year), **Projected Value** (year), **CAGR**
- Use consistent currency (typically USD for global, local currency for domestic)
- Cite the source for every data point with numbered references
- Note any significant methodology differences between sources

## Competitive Landscape Analysis

### Porter's Five Forces Assessment
1. **Threat of New Entrants** — capital requirements, regulatory barriers, brand loyalty, distribution access
2. **Bargaining Power of Suppliers** — supplier concentration, switching costs, input differentiation
3. **Bargaining Power of Buyers** — buyer concentration, price sensitivity, switching costs
4. **Threat of Substitutes** — alternative solutions, price-performance tradeoff
5. **Competitive Rivalry** — number of competitors, industry growth rate, differentiation, exit barriers

### Competitor Mapping
For each identified competitor tier:
- Company name and headquarters
- Market share estimate (if available)
- Key products/services
- Geographic presence
- Competitive advantages
- Recent strategic moves

### Competitive Positioning Matrix
Map competitors on two dimensions relevant to the industry, such as:
- Scale vs. Specialization
- Price vs. Quality
- Geographic reach vs. Market depth
- Technology capability vs. Cost competitiveness

## Industry Value Chain Analysis

Map the complete value chain from raw materials to end customer:
1. **Upstream** — raw materials, component suppliers
2. **Midstream** — manufacturing, assembly, integration
3. **Downstream** — distribution, retail, after-sales service

For each stage:
- Key players and concentration
- Value addition percentage
- Margin structure
- Integration trends (vertical/horizontal)

## PESTEL Analysis Framework

Evaluate macro-environmental factors:

| Factor | Areas to Analyze |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Political** | Trade policies, tariffs, government stability, political risk |
| **Economic** | GDP growth, interest rates, inflation, exchange rates, consumer spending |
| **Social** | Demographics, urbanization, lifestyle trends, health consciousness |
| **Technological** | Innovation pace, R&D spending, digital transformation, disruptive technologies |
| **Environmental** | Sustainability regulations, ESG requirements, circular economy, carbon targets |
| **Legal** | Data privacy (GDPR/CCPA), product safety, IP protection, employment law |

## Market Segmentation

### By Product/Service
- Define each segment clearly
- Quantify market share and growth rate
- Identify fastest-growing and most profitable segments

### By Geography
- Regional breakdown with market size and CAGR
- Identify fastest-growing regions
- Key characteristics of each regional market
- Present in summary table format:

| Region | 2024 Share | 2024A Value | 2034E Value | CAGR |
|--------|-----------|-------------|-------------|------|
| Region 1 | XX% | $XX.XB | $XX.XB | X.X% |
| ... | | | | |
| **Global Total** | **100%** | **$XX.XB** | **$XX.XB** | **X.X%** |

### By Customer Type
- Enterprise vs. consumer vs. government
- Industry verticals
- Company size (SME vs. large enterprise)

### By Distribution Channel
- Direct sales
- Distributors/resellers
- E-commerce
- OEM/white-label

## Growth Driver Analysis

For each identified growth driver:
1. **Describe the trend** with supporting data
2. **Quantify the impact** on market growth
3. **Assess durability** — is this a structural or cyclical driver?
4. **Company relevance** — how does this driver relate to the target company?

## Risk & Headwind Analysis

For each identified headwind:
1. **Describe the challenge** with specific examples
2. **Quantify the potential impact** (margin compression, demand reduction, cost increase)
3. **Assess probability and timing**
4. **Identify mitigation strategies** available to industry participants

## Charts and Visuals to Include

1. **Market size bar charts** — current vs. projected for key segments
2. **Regional distribution pie/bar chart**
3. **Growth driver summary** — visual ranking
4. **Competitive tier structure** — visual hierarchy
5. **Market segment comparison table**
6. **Geographic market comparison table**
