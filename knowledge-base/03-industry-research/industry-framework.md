# Industry Expert Report Framework

The Industry Expert Report identifies the specific industry/market of the customer company and provides comprehensive market research with analytical review. The focus is on the future market outlook and strategic positioning.

## Report Purpose

- Provide the customer with an independent, professional industry analysis
- Support underwriter/investor understanding of the market opportunity
- Contextualize the company within its competitive landscape
- Identify growth drivers, risks, and strategic opportunities

## Research Methodology

### Data Sources
1. **Market research reports** — Global Market Insights, DataMint Intelligence, Mordor Intelligence, Market Research Future, Statista, IBISWorld
2. **Financial databases** — Bloomberg, Refinitiv, S&P Capital IQ, PitchBook
3. **Regulatory publications** — SEC filings, exchange announcements, government statistics
4. **Industry associations** — trade body publications, conference proceedings
5. **News & media** — Financial Times, Bloomberg News, Reuters, industry-specific publications
6. **Academic research** — peer-reviewed journals, university research centers

### Research Process
1. Define the industry scope and classification (SIC/NAICS/GICS codes)
2. Identify macro market (broad category) and micro market (specific niche)
3. Quantify market size (current and projected) with CAGR
4. Map competitive landscape (tier structure, key players, market shares)
5. Analyze growth drivers and headwinds
6. Assess regulatory environment
7. Evaluate technology trends and disruption potential
8. Formulate strategic recommendations relevant to the company

### Data Validation
- **Triangulation** — cross-reference market data across at least 2-3 independent sources
- **Recency** — prioritize data from the last 12-18 months
- **Consensus validation** — note where sources diverge significantly
- **Transparency** — clearly cite all sources with numbered references

## Report Structure

### I. Scope of Services
Define what the report covers:
- Comprehensive industry overview
- Competitive landscape analysis
- Industry dynamics assessment
- Opportunity and risk identification
- Methodology and research credibility

### II. Executive Summary
- 3-5 key findings with supporting data points
- Market size headline (current value, projected value, CAGR)
- Regional growth highlights
- Emerging segment opportunities
- Competitive landscape summary

### III. Industry Overview and Market Context
1. **Market Definition and Scope** — what the industry encompasses, OEM/ODM dynamics if relevant
2. **Industry Strategic Importance** — role in broader ecosystem (technology gateway, supply chain integration, etc.)

### IV. Global Market Size and Trajectory
1. **Macro Market Context** — broader market the industry sits within
2. **Target Market Valuation and Growth** — specific market size, projected value, CAGR
3. **Market Composition by Category** — segment breakdown with market shares
4. **Emerging Product Categories** — high-growth subsegments above baseline
5. **Market Size Summary Table** — consolidated table with all market segments

### V. Geographic Market Distribution
1. **Regional Market Overview** — why geography matters for this industry
2. **Regional Market Analysis** — for each major region:
   - Market size (current and projected)
   - Market share percentage
   - CAGR
   - Key characteristics and growth drivers
   - Sub-regional dynamics
3. **Regional Distribution Table** — consolidated comparison table

### VI. Market Growth Drivers and Structural Tailwinds
Analyze 5-7 major growth drivers, such as:
- Demographic expansion and urbanization
- Rising disposable income and consumer spending
- Technological innovation (AI, IoT, 5G, etc.)
- Health and wellness consciousness
- Digital transformation and e-commerce growth
- Supply chain diversification
- Regulatory and ESG tailwinds

### VII. Market Segment Deep Dive
For each major product/service segment:
- Market characteristics and size
- Product category performance
- Growth drivers specific to segment
- Competitive positioning dynamics
- Technology integration trends

### VIII. Competitive Market Structure and Dynamics
1. **Industry Consolidation and Tier Structure**
   - Tier 1: Market leaders (market share, key players)
   - Tier 2: Regional/specialized players
   - Tier 3: Fragmented competitors
2. **Geographic Distribution** of manufacturing/operations
3. **Competitive Dynamics and Differentiation Factors**
   - Price competition
   - Quality and reliability
   - Time-to-market capability
   - Scale and cost advantages
   - Supply chain expertise
   - Customer relationships
4. **Barriers to Entry**
   - Capital requirements
   - Technical expertise
   - Regulatory certifications
   - Customer base development
   - Supply chain relationships

### IX. Industry Trends and Evolution
Identify 4-6 major trends shaping the industry, such as:
- Technology evolution (wireless, AI, smart integration)
- Premium segment growth
- Sustainability and ESG focus
- Supply chain diversification
- Regulatory changes

### X. Market Challenges and Headwinds
Analyze 4-6 major challenges:
- Supply chain vulnerabilities
- Labor and wage pressures
- Regulatory complexity
- Market consolidation and customer concentration
- Technology commoditization
- Macroeconomic sensitivity (cyclicality, interest rates, FX, geopolitics)

### XI. Market Outlook and Future Opportunities
1. Fundamental market characteristics supporting growth
2. Emerging product/service category opportunities
3. Geographic expansion opportunities
4. Technology and capability development opportunities
5. Industry consolidation outlook
6. Supply chain optimization opportunities

### XII. Strategic Recommendations
Company-specific recommendations across:
1. Competitive positioning strategy
2. Product/service portfolio strategy
3. Geographic expansion strategy
4. Capability development priorities

### XIII. Conclusion
- Summary of market outlook
- Key opportunities and risks
- Strategic positioning assessment

### XIV. References
Numbered reference list with full citations:
- [1] Source name. Report title. Date. Key data point.
- [2] ...

## Writing Style

- Professional, analytical tone suitable for board-level / investor audience
- Data-driven — every claim supported by cited market data
- Forward-looking emphasis — focus on market trajectory, not just current state
- Company-contextual — relate industry dynamics back to the target company's positioning
- Use charts and tables to present market data visually
- Include market size summary tables and regional distribution tables
