# Kick-off Meeting Deck Template

**Audience:** Customer (newly engaged client)
**Format:** PowerPoint (PPTX), 15-20 slides
**Purpose:** Align on engagement scope, timeline, deliverables, and data requirements at project kick-off

## Slide Structure

### Slide 1: Title Slide
- ORIONMANO logo
- "[Company Name] — Engagement Kick-off"
- "[Project Code Name]" (if applicable)
- Date
- "Strictly Private and Confidential"

### Slide 2: Agenda
1. Engagement overview and scope
2. Team introductions
3. Understanding your business
4. Work plan and timeline
5. Information requirements
6. Communication protocol
7. Q&A and next steps

### Slide 3: Engagement Overview
- Engagement type (DD, Valuation, Industry Report, Full Advisory)
- Purpose and objectives
- Target end-users of deliverables (underwriter, investor, board)

### Slide 4: Scope of Services
- Detailed breakdown of agreed scope:
  - A. Corporate Overview & Organization
  - B. Business Operations Analysis
  - C. Financial Statement & Accounting Policy Review
  - D. Internal Control & Risk Assessment
  - E. Targeted Procedures
- Reference to engagement letter

### Slide 5: Team Structure
- Orionmano engagement team:
  - Engagement Partner
  - Project Manager
  - Analysts
- Client-side counterparts:
  - Management sponsor
  - Finance team lead
  - Operations contact
  - Legal contact

### Slide 6: Your Company at a Glance
- Corporate structure diagram (from Stage 1 data)
- Key entities and their roles
- Jurisdictions

### Slide 7: Business Overview
- Principal activities
- Revenue segments
- Geographic presence
- Key metrics

### Slide 8: Engagement Phases
```
Phase 1: Data Collection & Setup (Week 1-2)
├── Information Request List (IRL)
├── Document upload & AI parsing
└── Initial management interviews

Phase 2: Analysis & Fieldwork (Week 3-6)
├── Financial statement analysis
├── Internal control evaluation
├── Industry research
├── Customer/supplier interviews
└── Site visits (if applicable)

Phase 3: Report Drafting (Week 5-8)
├── AI-generated draft sections
├── Senior review & refinement
└── Internal quality review

Phase 4: Finalization (Week 8-10)
├── Draft report to management
├── Management feedback & clarifications
├── Final report issuance
└── Presentation to stakeholders
```

### Slide 9: Detailed Timeline (Gantt Chart)
- Week-by-week breakdown with milestones
- Deliverable submission dates
- Key dependencies and client action items

### Slide 10: Information Request List Summary
- Categories of information needed:
  - Corporate documents
  - Financial statements and working papers
  - Tax filings and correspondence
  - Contracts and agreements
  - Legal documents
  - Operational data
  - Management representations
- Full IRL to be provided separately

### Slide 11: Interview Schedule
- Proposed interview list:
  - CEO/Founder — strategic vision, business development
  - CFO — financial performance, controls, tax
  - COO — operations, supply chain, HR
  - CTO — technology, systems, cybersecurity
  - Sales Director — customers, pipeline, market
  - Top 5 customers — satisfaction, continuity
  - Top 5 suppliers — relationship, terms
- Proposed dates and format (in-person vs. virtual)

### Slide 12: Site Visit Plan (if applicable)
- Locations to visit
- Objectives for each visit
- Schedule and logistics

### Slide 13: Communication Protocol
- Primary contacts on each side
- Regular status update cadence (weekly calls)
- Document sharing platform (secure portal)
- Escalation process for issues
- Confidentiality reminders

### Slide 14: Key Risks & Dependencies
- Client dependencies (document delivery, interview availability)
- Known challenges or complexities
- Mitigation strategies

### Slide 15: Deliverables Summary
| Deliverable | Target Date | Format | Recipient |
|-------------|-------------|--------|-----------|
| Industry Expert Report | Week X | PDF | Customer |
| Due Diligence Report (Draft) | Week Y | PDF | Customer/Underwriter |
| Valuation Report | Week Z | PDF | Customer/Underwriter/Investor |
| Company Teaser | Week Z+1 | PDF | Investor |
| Company Deck | Week Z+2 | PPTX | Investor |

### Slide 16: Immediate Next Steps
- [ ] Client to complete Stage 1 intake form
- [ ] Client to upload documents per IRL
- [ ] Schedule management interviews
- [ ] Confirm site visit dates
- [ ] Establish secure document sharing access

### Slide 17: Q&A
- Open discussion slide

## Design Guidelines
- Same brand guidelines as Sales Deck
- More detailed/text-heavy than Sales Deck (working document)
- Include page numbers and section headers
- Provide as leave-behind for client reference
