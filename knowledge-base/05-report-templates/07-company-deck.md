# Company Deck Template (Investor Presentation)

**Audience:** Investors (roadshow / investor meetings)
**Format:** PowerPoint (PPTX), 25-35 slides
**Purpose:** Comprehensive investor presentation for fundraising or IPO roadshow

## Slide Structure

### Section 1: Introduction (Slides 1-3)

**Slide 1: Title**
- Company logo + ORIONMANO "Prepared by" credit
- Company name and tagline
- "[IPO / Private Placement] Investor Presentation"
- Date
- Disclaimer: "Strictly Private and Confidential"

**Slide 2: Disclaimer**
- Forward-looking statements warning
- No-offer notice
- Information basis and limitations

**Slide 3: Table of Contents**

### Section 2: Investment Thesis (Slides 4-6)

**Slide 4: Investment Highlights**
- 5-6 key reasons to invest (icon + 1-2 sentence each):
  - Market leadership position
  - Attractive industry tailwinds
  - Proven revenue growth
  - Scalable business model
  - Experienced management team
  - Clear path to value creation

**Slide 5: Key Financial Metrics**
- Revenue, EBITDA, Net Income — 3-year trend with bar charts
- Key ratios: margins, growth rates, ROE

**Slide 6: Transaction Summary**
- Offering structure, size, use of proceeds, timeline

### Section 3: Company Overview (Slides 7-12)

**Slide 7: Company at a Glance**
- Snapshot: founded, HQ, employees, customers, revenue
- Visual timeline of key milestones

**Slide 8: Business Model**
- How the company makes money
- Revenue streams and their characteristics
- Value proposition diagram

**Slide 9: Products & Services**
- Product/service portfolio
- Key features and differentiators
- Customer segments served

**Slide 10: Corporate Structure**
- Group structure diagram
- Entity roles and jurisdictions

**Slide 11: Geographic Presence**
- Map visualization of operations and markets
- Revenue by geography

**Slide 12: Competitive Advantages / Moats**
- 4-5 sustainable competitive advantages
- Evidence for each (data points, customer testimonials, market position)

### Section 4: Industry & Market (Slides 13-16)

**Slide 13: Market Opportunity**
- TAM, SAM, SOM framework
- Market size and CAGR
- Key growth drivers

**Slide 14: Industry Dynamics**
- Market trends favoring the company
- Regulatory tailwinds
- Technology enablers

**Slide 15: Competitive Landscape**
- Competitive positioning matrix
- Market share data
- Key differentiators vs. peers

**Slide 16: Growth Strategy**
- Organic growth initiatives
- Geographic expansion plans
- Product/service roadmap
- M&A strategy (if applicable)

### Section 5: Financial Performance (Slides 17-23)

**Slide 17: Revenue Overview**
- 3-year revenue trend with growth rates
- Revenue by segment breakdown
- Revenue by type (recurring vs. one-off)

**Slide 18: Revenue Deep Dive**
- Unit economics (ARPU, churn, LTV, CAC)
- Customer metrics (total, new, retained)
- Pricing trends

**Slide 19: Profitability**
- Gross margin trend
- EBITDA margin trend
- Net margin trend
- Margin expansion drivers

**Slide 20: Balance Sheet Strength**
- Key balance sheet metrics
- Working capital position
- Debt profile and leverage ratios
- Cash position

**Slide 21: Cash Flow Generation**
- Operating cash flow trend
- Free cash flow trend
- Capex as % of revenue
- Cash conversion

**Slide 22: Financial Projections**
- 3-5 year revenue and EBITDA projections
- Key assumptions (brief)
- Scenario range

**Slide 23: Key Operating Metrics / KPI Dashboard**
- Industry-specific KPIs
- Operational efficiency metrics
- Customer/growth metrics

### Section 6: Management & Governance (Slides 24-26)

**Slide 24: Management Team**
- CEO, CFO, COO, CTO photos and brief bios
- Years of experience, notable achievements

**Slide 25: Board of Directors**
- Board composition
- Independent directors
- Committee structure

**Slide 26: Corporate Governance & ESG**
- Governance framework highlights
- ESG initiatives and achievements
- Compliance status

### Section 7: Transaction & Closing (Slides 27-30)

**Slide 27: Use of Proceeds**
- Pie chart showing allocation
- Strategic rationale for each use

**Slide 28: Valuation Context**
- Comparable company multiples
- Implied valuation range
- Premium/discount justification

**Slide 29: Risk Factors**
- Key risk factors (5-8 items)
- Mitigation strategies

**Slide 30: Why Invest — Summary**
- Compelling closing statement
- Reiterate 3-4 most compelling investment points
- Contact information

### Appendix Slides
- Detailed financial tables
- Comparable company details
- Additional market data
- Supplementary charts

## Design Guidelines
- Company branding with Orionmano "Prepared by" credit
- Professional, investor-grade design quality
- Data visualization: charts, graphs, infographics
- Consistent color scheme
- Minimal text — let data and visuals tell the story
- Every slide should answer "why should I invest?"
