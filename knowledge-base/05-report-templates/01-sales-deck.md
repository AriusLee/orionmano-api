# Sales Deck Template

**Audience:** Potential customer (company considering engaging Orionmano)
**Format:** PowerPoint (PPTX), 10-15 slides
**Purpose:** Present Orionmano's capabilities and proposed engagement to win the customer

## Slide Structure

### Slide 1: Title Slide
- ORIONMANO logo and "Assurance Services" tagline
- "[Company Name] — Advisory Engagement Proposal"
- Date
- "Strictly Private and Confidential"

### Slide 2: About Orionmano
- Company overview (Hong Kong-based financial advisory)
- Core services: Business valuation, Due diligence, Accounting & tax, Regulatory compliance
- Standards: IFRS 13 / ASC 820
- Key differentiator: AI-powered advisory platform

### Slide 3: Understanding Your Business
- Brief overview of the prospect's company (from Stage 1 intake)
- Industry positioning
- Key strengths identified

### Slide 4: Your Opportunity
- Market opportunity summary (high-level industry data)
- Strategic timing assessment
- Why now is the right time to engage

### Slide 5: Proposed Scope of Services
- Tailored service package based on prospect's needs:
  - Industry Expert Report
  - Due Diligence Report
  - Valuation Report
  - IPO readiness assessment (if applicable)
  - Investor materials preparation

### Slide 6: Our Approach
- AI-powered analysis + senior expert review
- Progressive engagement model (Stage 1 → 2 → 3)
- Data-driven, transparent methodology

### Slide 7: Deliverables
- List of specific deliverables with brief descriptions
- Sample page screenshots from redacted prior engagements
- Timeline for each deliverable

### Slide 8: Engagement Timeline
- Gantt-style timeline showing:
  - Data collection phases
  - Analysis and report drafting
  - Review and finalization
  - Key milestones

### Slide 9: Team & Credentials
- Engagement partner profile
- Key team members
- Relevant experience and credentials
- Notable past engagements (anonymized if needed)

### Slide 10: Case Studies (Optional)
- 1-2 anonymized examples of similar engagements
- Outcomes achieved

### Slide 11: Investment
- Fee structure and payment terms
- Scope inclusions and exclusions
- Value proposition justification

### Slide 12: Next Steps
- Proposed kickoff timeline
- Required information to begin
- Contact information
- Call to action

## Design Guidelines
- Use Orionmano brand colors (dark navy, white, teal accents)
- Clean, professional layout
- Minimal text per slide — use bullet points
- Include company logo on every slide
- "Strictly Private and Confidential" footer
