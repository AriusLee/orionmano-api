# Due Diligence Framework

This framework defines how Orionmano conducts financial due diligence engagements. The DD report is prepared for underwriter reference and assesses the target company's historical performance, financial position, internal controls, and risk factors.

> **Prerequisite:** A Gap Analysis (see `gap-analysis.md`) is typically completed before a full DD engagement begins. The gap analysis identifies high-level Nasdaq listing readiness gaps, which then inform the scope and focus areas of the DD. The standard engagement flow is: Gap Analysis → Mandate → DD + Industry Report → Valuation.

## Scope of Engagement

The DD engagement provides an independent, data-driven assessment covering:

### A. Corporate Overview & Organization
1. Trace company evolution from incorporation through latest period, highlighting milestones, pivots, and capital raises
2. Map current legal structure, equity ownership (ordinary, preference, convertible), and inter-company relationships within the group
3. Confirm statutory compliance status (relevant jurisdictions) and board/shareholder authorities

### B. Business Operations Analysis
1. **Segment Analysis** — For each revenue segment:
   - End-to-end workflow observation
   - Volume trend analysis (units, subscribers, churn, ARPU) by product class
   - Revenue recognition methodology and deferred obligations
   - Support-service SLAs and cost-to-serve metrics
2. **Competitive Positioning** — Benchmark pricing discipline and market share relative to peers
3. **Strategic Assessment** — Evaluate management's roadmap: expansion, diversification, ESG, digital transformation

### C. Financial Statement & Accounting Policy Review
1. Reconcile audited financial statements to trial balance; perform trend and ratio analysis (gross margin, OCF yield, leverage)
2. Evaluate adoption and impact of critical accounting policies:
   - Lease accounting (IFRS 16 / ASC 842 / MFRS 16)
   - Revenue recognition (IFRS 15 / ASC 606 / MFRS 15)
   - Expected credit loss (IFRS 9 / ASC 326)
3. Test material journal entries, manual adjustments, and closing schedules for completeness and accuracy
4. Validate management budgeting and forecasting methodology; sensitize projections against historical run-rates

### D. Internal Control & Risk Assessment
1. Walkthrough of revenue, procurement, treasury, fixed-asset, and financial-reporting cycles; document key control points and test design & operating effectiveness
2. Identify control deficiencies — missing documentation, SOX readiness gaps, IT general control weaknesses (IAM, DRP, cybersecurity)
3. Evaluate segregation of duties in high-risk areas (cash disbursement, journal entry posting)
4. Review fraud-risk management framework and whistleblower mechanisms

### E. Targeted Procedures
- **Contract Review** — analyze material customer/supplier contracts, bank facilities, HP/leaseback agreements, IP licenses
- **Debt & Liquidity** — inventory on/off-balance sheet borrowings; test covenant compliance; model pro-forma leverage post-transaction
- **Related-Party Transactions** — trace flow-of-funds, pricing, and approvals for all RPTs in review period
- **Customer/Supplier Interviews** — structured calls/site visits with top counterparties; corroborate satisfaction, payment discipline, continuity of supply
- **Management & Shareholder Background** — verify academic credentials, directorships, litigation, regulatory sanctions
- **Legal Proceedings** — open-source litigation searches; obtain management representations re: contingent liabilities
- **Tax Position** — reconcile CIT filings to FS; inspect correspondence with authorities; review loss carryforwards and transfer-pricing arrangements
- **Industry & Policy Scan** — assess impact of regulatory changes, industry incentives, and upcoming reforms on company's model

## Engagement Process

```
1. Engagement letter signed
   ↓
2. Information request list (IRL) issued to management
   ↓
3. Documents received & uploaded to platform
   ↓
4. AI parses documents → populates structured data
   ↓
5. Management interviews conducted
   ↓
6. AI generates draft DD report sections
   ↓
7. Senior reviewer edits & refines
   ↓
8. Draft circulated to management for fact-checking
   ↓
9. Management responses incorporated
   ↓
10. Final report issued (with [ ] items noted as outstanding)
```

## Time Period Coverage
- **Primary Period:** Fiscal years covered by audited financial statements (typically 2-3 years)
- **Supplementary Period:** Latest management accounts (LTM or interim period, unaudited)
- **Comparative Analysis:** Where relevant, prior year data for trend analysis

## Limitations & Restrictions
- Access to information controlled by company management
- No independent valuation of physical assets performed (unless separately engaged)
- Limited access to detailed IT system documentation
- Reliance on management representations for certain operational metrics
- Findings based solely on information available at time of report preparation
