# Internal Control Evaluation Framework

This framework guides the evaluation of internal controls across key business cycles. The output is a structured table format used in the DD report.

## Business Cycles Evaluated

1. Fixed Assets Management
2. Information Technology Controls
3. Treasury and Cash Management
4. Human Resources and Payroll
5. Inventory Management (where applicable)
6. Revenue and Accounts Receivable
7. Procurement and Accounts Payable
8. Financial Reporting Controls

## Table Format

For each business cycle, controls are evaluated in a table with columns:

| Column | Description |
|--------|------------|
| **Control Point** | The specific process or control being evaluated |
| **Risk** | What could go wrong if the control is absent or ineffective |
| **Control Target** | The objective of having the control in place |
| **Control Description** | What the control procedure should look like when properly implemented |
| **Evaluation** | Current state assessment — what is actually in place and any gaps identified |
| **Suggestion** | Recommended improvement or remediation action |

## Fixed Assets Management Cycle

### Control Points to Evaluate
- **Asset Acquisition Authorization** — approval thresholds, PO/receipt/invoice three-way matching
- **Asset Registration and Tagging** — unique ID assignment, asset register completeness, location tracking
- **Asset Disposal Authorization** — approval process, valuation for disposal, competitive bidding
- **Asset Condition Assessment** — inspection protocols, maintenance scheduling, impairment indicators
- **Inventory Counting** — physical count frequency, cycle counting, variance investigation
- **Inventory Valuation** — NRV testing, obsolescence assessment, technology obsolescence for IT equipment

## Information Technology Controls

### Control Points to Evaluate
- **User Access Management (IAM)** — role-based access, provisioning/deprovisioning, periodic reviews, terminated employee deactivation
- **Data Backup and Recovery** — daily automated backup, offsite storage, monthly restore testing, documented DRP
- **System Security Controls** — antivirus, firewall, MFA, penetration testing, cybersecurity framework
- **Change Management** — authorization process, testing requirements, segregation of dev/test/prod environments

## Treasury and Cash Management

### Control Points to Evaluate
- **Bank Reconciliations** — daily vs monthly frequency, independent review, zero-tolerance policy for outstanding items
- **Cash Receipts Controls** — same-day logging, segregation of handling and recording, independent verification
- **Payment Authorization** — dual-approval thresholds, supporting documentation requirements, electronic workflow audit trail
- **Investment Controls** — board-approved policy, committee approval, quarterly performance reporting

## Human Resources and Payroll

### Control Points to Evaluate
- **Employee Master Data** — onboarding verification, credential checks, periodic audit
- **Payroll Processing** — independent review, segregation of duties, management approval before payment
- **Time and Attendance** — electronic vs manual tracking, supervisor approval, biometric authentication
- **Payroll Tax Compliance** — automated vs manual calculations, statutory filing timeliness

## Financial Reporting Controls

### Control Points to Evaluate
- **Month-end Close Process** — documented checklist, timeline, responsibilities, management review
- **Journal Entry Controls** — supporting documentation, authorization for material/unusual entries, independent review threshold
- **Account Reconciliations** — standard templates, maximum reconciliation period, approval process
- **Financial Statement Reviews** — CFO/external accountant review, analytical review procedures, variance analysis

## Revenue Cycle Controls

### Control Points to Evaluate
- **Customer Onboarding** — credit assessment, KYC/AML screening, approval documentation
- **Contract Management** — standard terms, approval for non-standard terms, contract database
- **Invoice Generation** — automated vs manual, accuracy checks, timely issuance
- **Revenue Recognition** — policy compliance, cut-off testing, deferred revenue adequacy
- **Collections Management** — aging monitoring, dunning procedures, escalation process, write-off authorization

## Procurement Cycle Controls

### Control Points to Evaluate
- **Vendor Qualification** — business license verification, AML screening, credit checks, ESG questionnaire
- **Purchase Authorization** — approval matrix by value, segregation of requisition/approval/receipt
- **Goods Receipt** — inspection and quality checks, three-way matching (PO/GRN/invoice)
- **Payment Processing** — batch approval, supporting documentation, segregation of initiation/approval

## Evaluation Rating Scale

| Rating | Description |
|--------|------------|
| **Effective** | Control is well-designed and operating effectively; no significant gaps |
| **Partially Effective** | Control exists but has gaps in design or operation; improvement needed |
| **Ineffective** | Control is absent, poorly designed, or not operating; material weakness |

## SOX Readiness Assessment (for US-bound listings)

For companies targeting US listings (Nasdaq, NYSE), additionally evaluate:
- SOX Section 302 — CEO/CFO certification readiness
- SOX Section 404 — Internal controls over financial reporting (ICFR) documentation
- PCAOB audit readiness
- Entity-level controls (tone at the top, risk assessment, monitoring)
- IT general controls (ITGCs)
- Significant account and disclosure controls
