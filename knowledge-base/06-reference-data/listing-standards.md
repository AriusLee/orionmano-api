# Listing Standards Reference

US listing requirements for Orionmano advisory engagements. Orionmano's client base is Asia-Pacific companies pursuing **Nasdaq IPOs**; deliverables target US-exchange listing standards. Other jurisdictions (HKEX, Bursa Malaysia, SGX) are out of scope for the current engagement model.

## Nasdaq (United States)

### Nasdaq Global Select Market (highest tier)
| Requirement | Standard |
|-------------|---------|
| Pre-tax income | $11M aggregate (3 years), each year positive |
| Pre-tax income (recent) | $2.2M in 2 of most recent 3 fiscal years |
| Stockholders' equity | $30M minimum |
| Market value of listed securities | $75M |
| Bid price | $4.00 minimum |
| Market makers | 3-4 required |
| Round lot holders | 400 minimum |
| Operating history | 2 years |

### Nasdaq Global Market
Four alternative standards (meet one):
1. **Income Standard:** $1M pre-tax income in most recent fiscal year
2. **Equity Standard:** $30M stockholders' equity
3. **Market Value Standard:** $75M market value of listed securities
4. **Assets/Revenue Standard:** $75M total assets AND $75M total revenue

Common to all: $4.00 min bid, 400 shareholders, 3-4 market makers

| Phase | Duration | Activities |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Phase 1: Pre-Submission | Months 1–6 | Gap analysis, pre-audit, restructuring (BVI/Cayman), PCAOB audit, F-1 drafting, underwriter introduction |

| Phase 3: Post-Approval & Listing | Months 9–10 | Placing list (500-600 investors), roadshow (2-3 rounds), pricing, bell ceremony |

## NYSE (New York Stock Exchange)

### NYSE Listed (main board)
| Requirement | Standard |
|-------------|---------|
| Pre-tax income | $10M aggregate over last 3 years (each year positive, $2M in each of two most recent years) |
| Stockholders' equity | $4M (with cash flow alternative test) |
| Market cap | $200M (alternative test) |
| Round lot holders | 400 |
| Public shares | 1.1M |
| Market value of public shares | $40M (IPO) |
| Bid price | $4.00 minimum |

### NYSE American (formerly NYSE MKT — small-cap tier)
Four alternative standards (meet one):
1. **Pre-tax income standard:** $750K pre-tax income in most recent FY or 2 of last 3
2. **Market cap standard:** $50M market cap + $4M stockholders' equity
3. **Market value standard:** $75M market value of listed securities OR total assets + revenue
4. **Net assets standard:** $4M stockholders' equity + 3 years operating history

Common: 400 round-lot holders, $3 minimum bid (or $2 with $15M public float).

## US Listing Compliance

### SEC Registration
- **S-1** — domestic issuer registration statement
- **F-1** — foreign private issuer (FPI) registration statement
- **20-F** — FPI annual report (replaces 10-K)
- **6-K** — FPI interim/material event disclosure (replaces 10-Q / 8-K)
- **10-K / 10-Q / 8-K** — domestic issuer periodic and current reports

### Foreign Private Issuer (FPI) qualification
A non-US company qualifies as FPI unless **both** of:
- (a) more than 50% of voting securities held by US residents, **AND**
- (b) any of: majority of executive officers/directors are US citizens or residents, more than 50% of assets located in US, business administered principally in the US

If FPI: can use IFRS as issued by IASB (no US GAAP conversion required), home country corporate governance practice exemption (audit committee independence still required), Reg FD does NOT apply, files on F-1 / 20-F / 6-K.

### Sarbanes-Oxley
- **Section 302** — CEO/CFO certification of financial reports
- **Section 404(a)** — management assessment of ICFR (all issuers including EGCs)
- **Section 404(b)** — auditor attestation of ICFR (waived for EGCs and smaller reporting companies under Dodd-Frank)

### PCAOB-Audited Financial Statements
- 2-3 years of audited financials by a PCAOB-registered firm (Reg S-X Article 3)
- For acquired businesses materially significant under Rule 3-05, additional audited financials required
- Pro forma financial information per Article 11 if business combinations / dispositions affect comparability

### Other US-Specific Requirements
- **JOBS Act / Emerging Growth Company (EGC)** — issuers <$1.235B revenue can use confidential submission, 2 years audit (vs 3), reduced executive comp disclosure, exemption from 404(b) for up to 5 years
- **Reg FD** (domestic only) — selective disclosure rules
- **Rule 144 / 144A** — resale of restricted securities; relevant for pre-IPO private placement structuring
- **FINRA review** — underwriter compensation reasonableness review

## General IPO Readiness Checklist (Nasdaq-targeted)

### Financial Readiness
- [ ] 2-3 years PCAOB-audited financial statements (clean opinion)
- [ ] US GAAP or IFRS-as-issued-by-IASB (FPI) — no MFRS / local GAAP for filing
- [ ] Consistent accounting policies across years
- [ ] Revenue recognition policies reviewed and documented (ASC 606 / IFRS 15)
- [ ] Related party transactions at arm's length, fully disclosed per S-X 4-08(k)
- [ ] Working capital sufficiency for 12 months post-IPO (representation in F-1)
- [ ] Debt covenants compliant or waived for IPO use of proceeds
- [ ] Tax compliance up to date (US, home country, opco jurisdictions)
- [ ] Pro forma adjustments quantified for any restructuring / acquisitions

### Governance Readiness (Nasdaq Listing Rule 5605)
- [ ] Majority independent board (5605(b))
- [ ] Audit committee — fully independent, ≥3 members, financial expert (5605(c))
- [ ] Compensation committee — fully independent (5605(d))
- [ ] Nominating committee — fully independent (5605(e))
- [ ] Code of conduct (5610)
- [ ] Whistleblower policy (SOX 301 / Exchange Act 10A(m))
- [ ] D&O insurance in place
- [ ] FPI issuers may invoke home country practice exemption for board / committee structure (but NOT for audit committee independence)

### Operational Readiness
- [ ] Internal controls documented and tested for SOX 404
- [ ] IT general controls adequate (SOC 1 or equivalent)
- [ ] Financial reporting close process documented and timed (15-day close target)
- [ ] Key management team in place (CFO with US-listed company experience preferred, CS, GC)
- [ ] Investor relations function planned (IR firm or in-house)

### Legal Readiness
- [ ] Clean corporate structure (Cayman / BVI topco typical for non-US issuers)
- [ ] IP ownership confirmed and assigned to listing entity
- [ ] Material contracts reviewed for change-of-control and disclosure triggers
- [ ] No material litigation pending undisclosed
- [ ] Regulatory licenses and approvals current in all operating jurisdictions
- [ ] KYC/AML procedures in place for any consumer-facing operations
- [ ] Cayman / BVI legal opinions on enforceability / topco structure
