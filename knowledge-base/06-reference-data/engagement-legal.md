Standard legal language and templates used across Orionmano advisory deliverables.

## Engagement Letter Standard Clauses

We are pleased to submit our [report type] prepared in connection with
[Company Name] (the "Company"), in accordance with the terms of our
engagement agreement dated [date] (the "Contract").

We refer you to [relevant sections], which outline the scope of our
engagement, the sources of information relied upon, and the limitations
of the work performed.

```
The Executive Summary is intended to highlight the most significant
matters identified during our review in relation to the Services. It is
not exhaustive and should be read in conjunction with the full report
for a comprehensive understanding.

Financial analysis] reflects information available as of this date.
Subsequent developments may affect the continued relevance and
applicability of the findings presented herein.

```

time of the report's preparation. We accept no responsibility for
matters not addressed in this report or for information that may
subsequently become available.

confirmation. We therefore reserve the right to add to, delete from

## Standard Engagement Flow (Sales SOP)

The standard client engagement follows this process:

```
Phase 1: Introduction & Gap Analysis
  1.1  Send public company deck before first meeting
  1.2  Initial client meeting (60 min):
       - Company presentation (15-20 min, with case studies)
       - Needs assessment (20-30 min, pain points)
       - Request 4 key documents
  1.3  Client provides: corporate profile, 2-year audited financials,
       management reports, shareholder structure
  1.4  Deliver Gap Analysis report (Nasdaq IPO readiness assessment)
       ↓
Phase 2: Proposal & Mandate
  2.1  Second meeting — present gap analysis findings + IPO proposal
  2.2  Proposal includes: executive summary, methodology, timeline,
       resources, commercial terms (fee structure)
  2.3  Negotiate and sign Engagement Letter (Mandate Agreement)
       - Scope & deliverables
       - Roles & responsibilities
       - Confidentiality & IP
       - Termination & dispute resolution
  2.4  Invoicing on milestone basis
       ↓
Phase 3: IPO Execution (if mandated)
  3.1  Pre-audit & restructuring (BVI/Cayman structure, US GAAP conversion)
  3.2  PCAOB audit, F-1 drafting, underwriter introduction
  3.3  SEC filing, Q&A rounds, FINRA/Nasdaq review
  3.4  Roadshow, pricing, listing, bell ceremony
```

### Pre-IPO Financial Advisory Scope (per Mandate)
- IPO plan development and execution coordination
- Business valuation
- Accounting and financial review
- Listing suitability evaluation
- Corporate materials and prospectus coordination
- US financing channel advisory
- Coordination of underwriter, auditor, US/local counsel, IR, PCAOB, transfer agent, EDGAR filing
