# Tax & Regulatory Reference

Multi-jurisdiction tax and regulatory information commonly relevant to Orionmano advisory engagements.

## Malaysia

### Corporate Income Tax
- **Standard rate:** 24% for companies with paid-up capital > MYR 2.5 million
- **SME rates** (paid-up capital ≤ MYR 2.5M, gross income ≤ MYR 50M):
  - First MYR 150,000: 15%
  - MYR 150,001 - MYR 600,000: 17%
  - Above MYR 600,000: 24%
- **Tax year:** Follows financial year-end
- **Filing deadline:** Within 7 months of financial year-end
- **Estimated tax:** Monthly installments (CP204)

### Key Tax Incentives
- Pioneer Status (PS): Tax exemption on 70-100% of statutory income for 5-10 years
- Investment Tax Allowance (ITA): 60-100% of qualifying capex against statutory income
- Reinvestment Allowance (RA): For manufacturing companies reinvesting
- Digital economy incentives under MyDIGITAL
- Green technology incentives

### Tax Loss Carryforward
- Operating losses can be carried forward for 10 consecutive years (effective from YA 2019)
- No carryback allowed
- Unabsorbed capital allowances carried forward indefinitely (subject to conditions)

### Withholding Tax
- Dividends: 0% (single-tier system)
- Interest: 15% (non-resident)
- Royalties: 10% (non-resident)
- Technical/management fees: 10% (non-resident)
- Reduced rates under DTAs

### Transfer Pricing
- Arm's length principle applies
- Documentation required for related-party transactions
- Country-by-Country Reporting (CbCR) for groups with consolidated revenue ≥ MYR 3 billion
- Advance Pricing Arrangements (APA) available

### GST/SST
- Sales Tax: 5% or 10% on manufactured/imported goods
- Service Tax: 8% on prescribed services (effective March 2024)
- Exemptions for certain categories

## Hong Kong

### Corporate Tax
- **Standard rate:** 16.5%
- **Two-tier rates:**
  - First HKD 2 million: 8.25%
  - Remainder: 16.5%
- **Territorial basis:** Only Hong Kong-sourced income taxable
- No withholding tax on dividends
- No capital gains tax

### Key Features
- Simple, low-rate tax system
- No GST/VAT
- Generous deductions for capital expenditure
- DTAs with 45+ jurisdictions
- Offshore claims require substantiation

## Cayman Islands

### Tax-Exempt Status
- No corporate income tax
- No capital gains tax
- No withholding tax
- No estate/inheritance tax
- Tax Concessions Act: eligible companies can apply for 20-30 year tax exemption undertaking
- Popular jurisdiction for IPO holding companies

## British Virgin Islands (BVI)

### Tax-Exempt Status
- No corporate income tax
- No capital gains tax
- No withholding tax
- No inheritance tax
- Economic Substance Requirements (ESR) since 2019 — relevant activities must demonstrate adequate substance

## United States (for US listings)

### Corporate Tax
- **Federal rate:** 21% (flat)
- **State taxes:** Varies by state (0-12%)
- **Effective rate:** Typically 24-28% combined

### Key Considerations for Listing Companies
- ECI (Effectively Connected Income) for foreign companies with US operations
- FIRPTA for real property interests
- PFIC rules for passive foreign investment companies
- Subpart F income for controlled foreign corporations (CFCs)
- GILTI (Global Intangible Low-Taxed Income)
- FDII (Foreign-Derived Intangible Income) deduction

## Singapore

### Corporate Tax
- **Standard rate:** 17%
- **Partial exemption:** First SGD 200,000 at effective rates of 4.25-8.5%
- **No capital gains tax** (with exceptions)
- **Territorial basis** with broad treaty network
- **GST:** 9% (from January 2024)

## Regulatory Compliance

### KYC/AML Requirements
- Customer Due Diligence (CDD) — identity verification, beneficial ownership
- Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) — for high-risk customers, PEPs
- Ongoing monitoring — transaction monitoring, periodic review
- Suspicious Transaction Reporting (STR)
- Record keeping requirements (minimum 5-6 years depending on jurisdiction)
