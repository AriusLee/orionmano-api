# Accounting Standards Reference

Key accounting standards referenced in Orionmano advisory reports. This is not exhaustive — it highlights the standards most commonly relevant to DD and valuation work.

## Revenue Recognition

### IFRS 15 / MFRS 15 / ASC 606 — Revenue from Contracts with Customers

**Five-Step Model:**
1. Identify the contract with a customer
2. Identify the performance obligations in the contract
3. Determine the transaction price
4. Allocate the transaction price to performance obligations
5. Recognize revenue when (or as) performance obligations are satisfied

**Key Considerations for DD:**
- Point-in-time vs. over-time recognition
- Variable consideration (rebates, discounts, bonuses)
- Contract modifications
- Principal vs. agent assessment
- Franchise/license revenue — right-to-use vs. right-to-access
- Multiple-element arrangements
- Bill-and-hold arrangements

**Common DD Findings:**
- Premature revenue recognition (before performance obligation satisfied)
- Incorrect principal/agent classification
- Non-recurring revenue classified as recurring
- Deferred revenue adequacy
- Related-party revenue at non-market prices

## Lease Accounting

### IFRS 16 / MFRS 16 / ASC 842 — Leases

**Lessee Accounting:**
- Recognize right-of-use (ROU) asset and lease liability for all leases > 12 months
- Depreciate ROU asset over lease term
- Recognize interest on lease liability
- Exemptions: short-term leases (≤ 12 months), low-value assets

**Key Considerations for DD:**
- Lease classification (operating vs. finance under ASC 842)
- Discount rate selection (IBR vs. rate implicit in lease)
- Sale-and-leaseback transactions — classification and gain recognition
- Lease modifications and reassessments
- Variable lease payments
- Impact on leverage ratios and EBITDA

**Common DD Findings:**
- Leases not captured on balance sheet
- Incorrect discount rate
- Leaseback transactions used for off-balance sheet financing
- Substance-over-form issues in sale-leaseback

## Financial Instruments & Expected Credit Loss

### IFRS 9 / MFRS 9 / ASC 326 — Financial Instruments / Credit Losses

**ECL Model (IFRS 9):**
- Stage 1: 12-month ECL (performing)
- Stage 2: Lifetime ECL (significant increase in credit risk)
- Stage 3: Lifetime ECL (credit-impaired)

**Key Considerations for DD:**
- ECL provision adequacy vs. historical loss experience
- Aging analysis and concentration assessment
- Forward-looking information in ECL model
- Related-party receivable provisioning
- Convertible instrument classification (debt vs. equity vs. compound)

**Common DD Findings:**
- Underprovision for ECL, especially on concentrated receivables
- Missing forward-looking adjustment
- Convertible instruments misclassified

## Fair Value Measurement

### IFRS 13 / ASC 820 — Fair Value Measurement

**Fair Value Hierarchy:**
- **Level 1:** Quoted prices in active markets for identical assets/liabilities
- **Level 2:** Observable inputs other than Level 1 (comparable transactions, yield curves)
- **Level 3:** Unobservable inputs (management assumptions, DCF models)

**Application in Valuation Reports:**
- Business valuations typically Level 3
- Comparable company analysis may use Level 1/2 inputs
- All significant assumptions must be disclosed
- Sensitivity analysis on Level 3 inputs required

## Impairment

### IAS 36 / MFRS 136 / ASC 350, 360 — Impairment of Assets

**Impairment Indicators:**
- Decline in market value
- Adverse changes in technology, markets, economy, laws
- Increases in market interest rates
- Net assets exceed market capitalization
- Asset damage, obsolescence, or idle capacity
- Restructuring plans
- Worse-than-expected asset performance

**Key Considerations for DD:**
- PPE impairment testing methodology
- Goodwill impairment (annual testing)
- Intangible assets with indefinite useful lives
- CGU identification and allocation
- Recoverable amount calculation (higher of fair value less costs of disposal, value in use)

## Presentation and Disclosure

### IAS 1 / MFRS 101 / ASC 205-235 — Presentation of Financial Statements

**Key Considerations for DD:**
- Current vs. non-current classification
- Material misclassifications (e.g., trade payables classified as bank borrowings)
- Consistency of presentation year-over-year
- Adequate disclosure of accounting policies
- Related party disclosures (IAS 24 / MFRS 124)

## Consolidation

### IFRS 10 / MFRS 10 / ASC 810 — Consolidated Financial Statements

**Key Considerations for DD:**
- Correct identification of subsidiaries (control assessment)
- Variable Interest Entities (VIEs) under US GAAP
- Non-controlling interests treatment
- Intercompany elimination completeness
- Foreign subsidiary translation (IAS 21 / ASC 830)
